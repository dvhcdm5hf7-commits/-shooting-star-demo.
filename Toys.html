THE SPINNING SPIRAL

Handcrafted kinetic copper art

Copper in Motion

The Spinning Spiral transforms handcrafted copper into movement. Its changing form, reflections and shadows make the piece appear different from every angle as it turns.

Created individually by the coppersmith, each piece carries the subtle variations of genuine handwork rather than the uniformity of mass production.

MORE THAN METAL

At Shooting Star, copper is shaped by hand into objects meant to be touched, enjoyed and remembered. The hand of the artisan gives every piece its own character.
